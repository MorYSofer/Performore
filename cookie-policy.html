Cookie Policy

What cookies performore.co uses, why we use them, and how you can manage your preferences.

Last updated 19 July 2026
01

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They're widely used to make websites work, work more efficiently, and to provide information to the site owner. This policy explains how MSMC Limited Ltd, trading as Performore, uses cookies on performore.co (the "Site").

02

How we use cookies

performore.co uses cookies and similar technologies for a few purposes: to support essential functionality such as booking a call with us and loading our typography correctly, and — through Google Tag Manager — to understand how the Site is used and to measure the performance of our advertising with analytics and advertising partners such as Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, Reddit and Microsoft (Bing) Ads.

03

Types of cookies we use

Category Purpose Example
Strictly necessary Required for the Site to function correctly Session and security cookies set by your browser
Functional Enable enhanced functionality, such as scheduling a call Cookies set by Cal.com when you use our booking widget
Content delivery Load fonts and script libraries reliably Requests to Google Fonts and the jsDelivr CDN
Analytics Understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it Google Analytics, deployed via Google Tag Manager
Advertising / targeting Measure and improve the performance of our advertising, and, where applicable, show more relevant ads Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, Reddit and Microsoft (Bing) Ads tags, deployed via Google Tag Manager

Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before non-essential analytics or advertising cookies are set.

04

Third-party cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services embedded in our Site, via Google Tag Manager (GTM) — a tag management system that loads the tags below on our behalf:

  • Cal.com — powers our call-booking widget and may set cookies to remember your scheduling preferences and prevent abuse.
  • Google Fonts — used to display our typography; loading fonts may involve a connection to Google's servers.
  • jsDelivr (CDN) — delivers script libraries, such as GSAP, that power animations on the Site.
  • Google Analytics — measures Site traffic and usage.
  • Google Ads — measures the performance of our Google advertising.
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Pixel — measures the performance of our Meta advertising.
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag — measures the performance of our LinkedIn advertising.
  • Reddit Pixel — measures the performance of our Reddit advertising.
  • Microsoft (Bing) Ads / UET — measures the performance of our Microsoft advertising.

These providers act independently, and their use of cookies is governed by their own privacy and cookie policies. We may add or remove tags from Google Tag Manager over time as our marketing and analytics setup evolves; this policy is kept up to date to reflect what's actually running on the Site.

05

Managing cookies

Most browsers let you control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting them. Because some cookies are required for core functionality — such as booking a call — disabling them may affect how parts of the Site work. You can manage cookie settings in your browser:

  • Google Chrome
  • Safari
  • Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge

Opting out of analytics and advertising cookies specifically

You can also manage or opt out of tracking directly with our analytics and advertising partners:

  • Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on
  • Google Ads Settings
  • Meta ad preferences
  • LinkedIn advertising settings
  • Reddit privacy and ad settings
  • Microsoft (Bing) personalised ads settings
  • Your Online Choices — an industry-wide opt-out for many advertising networks
06

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use or for legal reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.

07
